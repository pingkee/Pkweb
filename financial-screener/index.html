      How the rankings work

      Composite score

      Each metric is converted to a percentile rank from 0 to 100 among the stocks that pass the screen's hard filters. Lower-is-better metrics are reversed. The weighted average is the score you see. Missing metrics are skipped and the remaining weights are renormalized, so a partial record is not treated as a zero. Ties break on the criteria in the order they appear.

      Hard filters

      High Growth keeps only the growth universe with positive revenue growth on the chosen basis. Net-Growth requires positive shareholder equity growth and a falling 5-year share count. Net-Net has no hard filter. Weights change the order, never the gates.

      Data and signs

      Share-count growth is positive for dilution and negative for buybacks. This is the yfinance/GARP convention used by the scraper. Imports merge by ticker, keeping the row with the latest As_Of. Files are parsed locally; only the optional Save to data/ action sends the rows you choose to commit to your own repository. Yahoo data can be stale or incomplete; this is not investment advice.
