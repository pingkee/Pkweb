About

Programme leadership, with an engineer's hands.

I'm a Programme Manager at DSTA, where I lead classified programmes spanning web applications, backend services, mobile applications, and AI products.

My AI work covers LLM-powered products and inference: vLLM, Infinity, Ollama, and ComfyUI, along with fine-tuned offline classification models and high-throughput deployments built to meet client demand. I also work with agentic AI frameworks such as LangChain for tool use, retrieval, and multi-step orchestration.

I started as a full-stack developer, building React and TypeScript front ends, Node.js and FeathersJS services, and React Native apps deployed to AWS, the App Store, and Google Play. That hands-on background still shapes how I run programmes today.

As a Scrum Alliance certified ScrumMaster, I enjoy turning ambiguous problems into clear, deliverable plans. Outside work, I build web and mobile apps for fun and keep learning by shipping.
